## 6. Phase geometry, events and evidence authority

- Event types (v1): `batch_started`, `backward_completed`, `physical_child_stepped`, `physical_scheduler_stepped`, `checkpoint_saved`, `checkpoint_loaded`, `train_completed`. - Per effective step require ordered Batch→Backward pairs, correct sample uniqueness, exactly one physical Step per required child and Scheduler, actual change where required and post-save binding. R2 no-sync state is verified by **next** Batch-start fingerprints. Resume requires `checkpoint_loaded` before any training Batch. Expected target save steps: Fresh=1, Resume=2; Fresh Step-2 may appear as an additional non-handoff event.

Fix D separately correlates immutable tickets, subprocess exit status, physical evidence snapshots, source/submodule/environment identities and completed checkpoint files.
- `publish_phase_result` remains no-overwrite and status limited to `incomplete|fail|worker_complete`; after the entire lease exits, the Worker may publish `worker_complete` with its evidence digest only. Neither C0 nor C1 changes a production qualification row.
